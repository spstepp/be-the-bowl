The three parts

Keep these separate. Confusion begins when they are treated as one thing.

The bowl

The bowl is you. Form, capacity, worth. A poor note does not crack it.

The striker

The striker is thought. The sentence you allow to land. Neglect is a way of choosing.

The tone

The tone is what follows: feeling, then conduct, then the effect in the room. Consequence, not identity.
